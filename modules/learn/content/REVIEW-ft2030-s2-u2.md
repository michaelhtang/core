## Definitions and rules in our words, for Mr. Tang to confirm

12. Fixed cost: "stays the same however many items are sold". Variable cost: "grows with each item sold" (the FT10 wording).
13. Unit cost: "the variable cost of one item: what it costs to make and sell one". The outline (Lesson 2, slide 9) says "what one item costs you in materials"; ours is the variable cost per item, so packaging and a per-sale fee count, as the outline's Lesson 6 notes say.
14. Contribution margin: the price minus the variable cost of one item; "what each sale puts toward the fixed costs". Not profit.
15. Break-even point: "the number of items a business must sell for its sales to cover all of its costs" (the FT10 wording); fixed costs divided by the contribution margin.
16. Total cost = fixed costs + unit cost x items sold (level 1 card 6).
17. Unsold items: items made and not sold are a cost already paid (level 3 card 6). The two ways named, a discount on the last day and smaller batches made again when stock runs out, come from the outline's Lesson 5 list of answers; the outline's other two (donating, pre-selling) are not used.
18. "Four in five must buy" (level 3 card 2) is plain arithmetic, 40 of 50 is 80%; l3-q3 uses a different case, 30 of 50 is 60%. We do not claim it is impossible.
