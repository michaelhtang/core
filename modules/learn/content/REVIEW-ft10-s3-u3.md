## The minimum-payment rule and the payoff figures

1. **The rule: 3% of the balance, never under $10.00, rounded up to the cent, never more than the balance.** Source: Lesson 19 outline, line 219 ("20.99%, 3% minimum with a $10 floor") and line 101; the preview's table (line 19) says 3%. The game uses the same rule (`MIN_PAYMENT` in `modules/credit/constants.js`). Level 3 card 2 and every minimum in levels 3, 4 and 6. The outline states no rounding; the unit uses the game's (minimum rounded up, interest rounded down to the cent).
2. **Month-by-month order.** The unit uses the game's order everywhere: interest for the month is the whole statement balance x rate / 12, the payment comes off, and the interest is added to what is left (new balance = balance - payment + interest). A real card usually counts interest day by day, so real figures differ a little. The game's own doc says the same (`docs/credit.md`, line 43). Level 2 card 3 and level 6 card 4 say so.
3. **Your slide's payoff figures differ from the table.** Slide 5 (line 95) shows "about 21 years and $6,400 in interest" for the minimum only, and "34 months and about $1,600" for $200 a month; its speaker note (line 101) calls them an estimate. Recomputed with the rule above, $5,000.00 at 20.99%: minimum only is 267 months (about 22 years) and $6,692.56 interest; $200.00 a month is 34 months and $1,631.26 interest (total paid $6,631.26). The 34 months matches your slide. The unit uses mine (level 4 card 4, ft10-s3-u3-l4-q1, -q2, -q6).
4. **The minimum-only figures are the game's, with one quirk.** The numbers for minimum only ($5,000.00: 267 months, $6,692.56; $1,000.00: 139 months, $1,101.28, in level 6 card 5) are what the Credit Bureau's payoff calculator prints, so a student's own screen agrees (`payoff()` in `modules/credit/score.js`). The calculator adds interest even on the last statement, where the minimum equals the whole balance. In the real monthly tick that last statement is paid in full and is not charged interest, so a card played to the end would finish one month earlier (266 months, $6,692.50 interest). The difference is one month and six cents; the questions do not depend on it. It may be a small quirk in the game; I did not change the game.
5. **First-month interest on $5,000.00 is $87.45 in the tables** (the game drops the last part of a cent; to the nearest cent it is $87.46). ft10-s3-u3-l4-q4 accepts either.
6. **$200.00 a month has no game equivalent** (the calculator only does the minimum). That row is my table, same rule, with the last payment smaller than $200.00.
