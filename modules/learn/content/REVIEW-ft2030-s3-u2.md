## Definitions and rules in our words, for Mr. Tang to confirm

14. **Risk tolerance** (level 1 card 2): "how big a fall in value a person can live with, without being forced to sell or choosing to sell in a panic". It belongs to the person, not the investment. **`ft2030-s3-u1` only names risk tolerance and was still being written when this unit was written, so this unit defines it; please check the two use the same wording.**
15. **The three things that shape it: time, need, reaction** (level 1 cards 3 to 6 and the table). The plan's three, matching the FinTech 10 "four questions" (`ft10-s2-u4` level 6: time horizon, how much do I need, how will I feel). The table's "more room" and "less room" are leanings, not rules, and the card says they decide nothing for a person.
16. **A fall is a loss on paper until you sell** (level 1 card 5, question 4): our wording; it agrees with `ft10-s2-u4` level 7 (Priya "locked in a loss" by selling).
17. **Rise needed to get back = dollars lost divided by dollars left** (level 2 card 4, table in card 5, `ft2030-s3-u2-l2-q4`, `-l2-q5` and `-l3-q10`): arithmetic, ours. The table's rises are rounded to one decimal place. This extends `ft10-s2-u4` level 5 card 6 (a fall of 30% needs about 42.9%); that figure is not repeated here.
18. **"Moves stack": two falls of 10% leave 81%, not 80%** (level 2 card 6, questions 3 and 7): arithmetic, ours.
19. **The four phases in plain words** (level 3 card 1): expansion, growing with more jobs and more spending; peak, the top where growth stops and turns; contraction, shrinking with fewer jobs and less spending; trough, the bottom where it turns up. Ours.
20. **Pullback, correction and bear market** (level 3 card 4): see "Where the outline's own figures are used" for the one added phrase.
21. **Trend, support and resistance** (level 5 cards 1 and 2): trend is the way the line is mostly heading (up, down or sideways); support is a price the line keeps bouncing up from, like a floor; resistance is a price the line keeps failing to climb past, like a ceiling; both are drawn afterwards. Ours.
22. **"A coin has no memory, so after any run of heads the next flip is still even"** (level 5 card 4, question 4): a statement about a fair coin, ours. The made-up price is a toy: real prices are not coin flips (card 5).
23. **Affiliate link** (level 4 card 4): "a link that pays the sender when you sign up or buy". Ours.
24. **A fee-only planner** (level 4 cards 2 and 3): paid by the client, a flat fee or by the hour, with no product paying the planner. The outline's own description ("flat fee or hourly, no product commission", line 215); the term "fee-only" is the outline's.
