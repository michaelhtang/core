# FinWord word list: for Mr. Tang to review

How the daily word is picked: every day each course gets one word. Count the days since 1 January 2026 (Edmonton date), divide by the number of words in that course's list, and take the word at the remainder. So the list below is played in order, one row a day, and starts again at the top when it reaches the end. Everyone in the same course gets the same word on the same day. Students see only the section name as a hint; after they win or lose they see the word, the full term and its meaning. Finishing the day's game, win or lose, counts for Today's errand "Play today's FinWord" (50 cents, once, only when it is one of that day's picks); winning pays nothing extra. Every term and meaning is copied word for word from the lesson files. To remove a word, delete its line in `modules/arcade/wordle-words.js`; to add one, add a line (4 to 9 letters, A to Z only). Deleting or adding a line moves every later day along by one, and a game a student already started keeps its own word. The rows here are in play order, so check the order too.

Only words from lessons that are on the site are used. A word from a section that is not live yet (FinTech Marketplace; The Venture and The Investor) joins the game by itself when those lessons go live.
