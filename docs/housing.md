# housing (Wave 2b): rent, buy, mortgage

Code: `modules/housing/` (`constants.js` every number with its source and checked date, `mortgage.js` pure maths, `index.js` module). Tests: `test/mortgage.test.js` (maths, two hand-worked cases), `test/housing.test.js` (module over HTTP). Config order: `bank-basic, rates, jobs, housing, credit, bills, (insurance,) savings`. Events in: `module.jobs.paid`, `module.rates.changed`, `module.rates.pending`, `module.credit.statement`, `world.closed`.

## Tenure
`parents` (living with parents: a $400 monthly household contribution instead of rent, no deposit, line `housing-rent` category needs; illustrative, anchored on the Globe and Mail 2023 poll where 52% of rent-charging parents ask $500 or less), `shared` (no row: bills' own rent line), `apt1`, `apt2`, `apt2-roommate` ("2-bedroom with a roommate": half the 2-bedroom rent, deposit = that half), or `own`. A move applies at the next tick; the damage deposit (one month's rent, ledger account kind `deposit`) is taken when you ask and refunded or trimmed when the move takes effect. Renters and owners get `module.housing.costs` every tick; bills turns the lines into `housing-<key>` due lines and stops charging its own rent.

## Two properties
The Buy tab lets the student choose: `average` (Edmonton average condo, $202,000, fee $400, heating $60; `options.housing.condoPriceCents` / `condoFeeCents` / `heatingCents`) or `starter` (older Edmonton 1-bedroom, $150,000, fee $546 including heat so heating $0; `starterPriceCents` / `starterFeeCents`). Source: 2026 Edmonton listings seen 2026-10-22, for example a 1970-built 778 sq ft 1-bedroom at 9725 106 Street listed at $149,900 with a $546/month fee covering heat, water and electricity (rew.ca/properties/1002-9725-106-street-edmonton-ab confirmed size and year; price and fee from the same listing via search). Qualification, CMHC, closing costs and mortgage maths are the same; `housing_homes.property` records which one was bought and its cost lines use that property's fee.

## The mortgage is not a bill
Housing debits it at its own tick (`housing.mortgage_payment`: chequing -payment, mortgage +principal, sink +interest). It runs after jobs pay and before credit and bills. A payment chequing cannot cover is missed: that month's interest is added to the debt, the payment goes to arrears, a $45 NSF fee line goes to bills (category `fees`, so it earns no late fee), `module.housing.payment {onTime:false}` fires (credit marks it late). The next debit that can carry payment plus arrears clears both; `/catch-up` clears arrears at any time (all principal, since the interest was already added). Three missed in a row: a teacher warning. No foreclosure.

Maths: monthly rate `i = (1 + r/2)^(1/6) - 1` (semi-annual compounding), payment rounded to the cent, interest each tick `round(balance * i)`, the last payment clears the balance. Qualifying: the stress rate `max(contract + 2%, 5.25%)`; GDS = (stress payment + property tax + heating + 50% condo fee) / gross, limit 39%; TDS adds the card minimum (`module.credit.statement`), limit 44%. Gross = average of the last 3 months of `module.jobs.paid`. Premium by loan-to-value from CMHC's table (+0.20% for 30 years), added to the loan. 30-year amortization: offered on an insured purchase to a first-time buyer (no home yet this season) and on any uninsured one.

Cash at closing: down payment + $1,500 legal + Alberta Land Titles ($50 + $5 per $5,000 or part) on the transfer and on the mortgage. The whole price, premium and closing costs go to the sink (the seller, insurer and lawyer are outside the town); a sale pays `value - 4% - legal - mortgage` from the mint (the buyer is outside the town too).

## An announced rate is priced in
While a policy-rate change is queued (`module.rates.pending`, kept in `housing_rates_pending` per world), quotes and new purchases use the 5-year fixed of the pending rate (`mortgage5yBp` on the event, rates' own `derive()`), and the stress test follows it. The quote says "Lenders have already priced in the Bank of DAA's announced rate" (`pricedIn`, `rateNote`). Otherwise a student who saw the announcement could buy before the tick and lock the old rate for the whole term (with +275 bp queued: $312 a month less, about $25,800 over the 5-year term, and an easier stress test). A cancelled change (`pendingBp: null`) or the tick that applies it removes the row; a new season starts with none (rows are per world). Existing mortgages and renewals keep the rate in force (`mortgage_bp` in the state) until their renewal, as before. Test: `test/housing.test.js` "an announced policy rate is priced into new mortgages at once ...".

## Renewal
When `term_months` (60, `options.housing.termMonths` overrides it for a class demo) have passed since purchase or the last renewal, the rate becomes rates' current 5-year fixed and the payment is recalculated on the balance over the amortization left. Old and new payments are stored (`housing_renewals`) and shown.

## Value, net worth
`module.housing.value` each tick: the value drifts +0.25% a month (`options.housing.driftBp`); the world teacher's market event moves every home +/-5 to 15% at once and announces the new values. Savings keeps the latest per (player, world) in `savings_home` and adds it to net worth and its snapshots; the mortgage is already negative in the ledger.

## Seasons
Tenure rows and homes carry `world_id`; every query uses the current world. Season close zeroes every player account (mortgage and deposit included) as `core.season_reset`, so a home must not survive: the `world.closed` listener marks the season's homes `ended`, and adds each owner's home value to their `season_results.net_worth_cents` (the core sums ledger balances only, so without this a homeowner's season result would be cash minus debt with no house; this is the one core table housing writes). In the new world nobody has a tenure row (a shared room) or a home, savings' `savings_home` is per world, and a student can buy again as a first-time buyer.

## Student landlords (6b)
A student may own a `primary` and one `rental` (`housing_homes.role`, unique per role among owned homes). The rental cannot be a first home, needs 20% down (uninsured), and the lender's TDS counts the primary's mortgage payment as a debt and half the NPC rent for that property as income. Each home has its own mortgage account (`mortgage` for the primary, `mortgage:<homeId>` for a rental), its own payment, renewal, arrears and owner lines (a rental's keys end in `-rental`), every month, let or vacant, whatever the owner's tenure. A rental may be listed to classmates (same class) at 60-150% of the NPC tier (starter vs the 1-bedroom, average vs the 2-bedroom, both at today's prices). A tenant asks for `unit:<homeId>` (reserved at once, moves next month, no deposit, must own no home); housing posts the rent tenant -> landlord at each tick before the homes (kind `housing.rent`, not a bill; bills' shared-room rent is not charged: the `housed` flag). Short: arrears, a $45 NSF fee (to the sink from chequing, else added to the arrears), credit late, stress +20; three missed in a row: eviction to the shared room, arrears written off. Rent changes need two months' notice when someone lives there; the landlord ends a tenancy with one month's notice; a sale ends it at once. Details and numbers: docs/phase6-roles.md "As built (6b landlords)".

## Not built / simplifications
No prepayment penalty on selling, no variable rate, no CMHC credit-score minimum (600) check, no foreclosure. Condo fees, heating, insurance and legal fees are illustrative. At most two homes (a primary and a rental).
