# Jobs: raises and promotions (Phase 2, decision 2026-10-22)

Pay is stored per student (`jobs_holdings`), not in the catalogue, so raises are individual. `payroll.js` (CRA T4127) is untouched: only the gross it is given changes.

## Raises
- After every 12 world months in the same job, step or since the last raise (the 13th pay after taking a job), payday raises the hourly wage by `options.jobs.raiseBp` (default 300 = 3%), rounded to the cent. Raises compound. A promotion or a teacher raise restarts the 12-month clock.
- Source for the default: Job Bank, Economic Scan - Alberta 2026 (https://on.jobbank.gc.ca/trend-analysis/job-market-reports/alberta/environmental-scan): average hourly wage of employees about $37.35 in 2025, up $0.97 (about 2.7%) from 2024, from the Labour Force Survey; Eckler 2026 Compensation Planning Survey, Alberta base-salary increase projection 3.4% (https://dailyhive.com/edmonton/alberta-salary-increase-wage-growth-report); Mercer 2026 Canada merit increases averaged 3.0%. Checked 2026-10-22 through web search results, not by opening the Statistics Canada tables: treat 3% as a rounded, realistic figure.

## Promotions
- Each catalogue job has 1 to 3 steps (`jobs_steps`; step 0 is the catalogue row). Rule for wages (2023 Alberta Wage and Salary Survey, ALIS, checked 2026-10-22): a step in a new occupation starts at that occupation's ALIS "Starting" average; the same occupation with experience uses its "Overall" (or "Top") average. Where ALIS has no profile for the role (warehouse lead hand, child care supervisor, office manager, customer service team lead, crew supervisor) the row says which cell of which profile stands in. Hours = ALIS weekly hours x 52 / 12, kept to 80..160 (retail store manager hours are not published: 40 h/week assumed).
- A student may apply once they have been 12 months in the step (`options.jobs.promotionMonths`) with a 10 to 500 character reflection (stored, shown to the teacher). The teacher approves or denies. After a denial the student waits 3 months (`reapplyMonths`). Teachers can also promote or raise at any time ("grant"), ignoring the clock.
- Approval changes title and wage at once in the holding; money moves at payday, so it is paid from the next payday. The wage never goes down: a promoted student keeps an earned raise if it is above the new step's wage. Hours come from the step. The employer stays the same fictional business.
- The pay stub keeps the title, wage and hours it was paid at, and shows a note the first month a raise or promotion applies.
- Switching job or quitting voids a waiting application; picking or assigning the job you already hold changes nothing.
- **Returning to a job (decision 2026-10-23, review B17).** A genuinely new job starts at catalogue (or step) wage x price index. Re-hired into a job held within the last 12 world months, a student is a returning employee, not a new hire: the previous wage, step and clocks come back (`jobs_past`), with the raise, promotion and first-raise clocks moved on by the time away (they pause while gone). Without this, switching A -> B -> A re-hired at catalogue x index, which beat keeping the job whenever inflation was above the raise (2507 vs 2347 cents/hour at 10% inflation after month 13). Rejected: max(catalogue x index, previous wage), which still rewards the switch at high inflation. Left more than 12 months ago: a new hire again. Season close clears `jobs_past` (world months restart).
  - Edge cases, both only when the teacher sets inflation above the yearly raise (default 3%): (1) a student away from a job for more than 12 months comes back as a new hire at catalogue x index, which can be more than a colleague who stayed and only had raises; (2) a student returning inside the 12 months gets their old wage, which can be less than a new hire's catalogue x index. Both are accepted: the window is what stops the same-month A -> B -> A switch, and max(previous, catalogue x index) would bring that exploit back. Being out of work for a year costs a year of pay, so (1) is not a profitable strategy.

## Season close
Season close (the `seasonClose` hook, inside the close transaction, so a failure fails the close rather than carrying wages into the next season) resets every holding to the catalogue entry (step 0, no raise, `since_month` 1) and voids waiting applications. Everyone keeps their job and starts the new season on the entry wage.

## Sim
Eligible bots apply (through the same functions as `POST /apply` and `/decide`); 10% of applications are approved on the spot (`options.jobs.simApprovePct`; it was 70% until the Phase 2 balance pass; deterministic hash, no teacher), the rest denied (3-month cool-off). `npm run sim -- --months=60` prints median gross at months 12/24/36/60.
