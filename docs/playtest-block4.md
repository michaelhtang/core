# Playtest: Block 4, current build (plan v2 wave 0)

One period, 20 minutes of play, nothing built for it. The point is to watch, not to teach.

## Before
- Season running on the local server; one world, Block 4 as a class; PIN cards printed (teacher console → class → cards).
- Real devices: the Chromebook cart and phones, not the projector. Pace: 1 month per day (default).
- Have the teacher console open on your laptop: Today and Town Walk panels.

## During (say as little as possible)
| Minute | You | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | "Log in with your card. This is your life at 22. See what you can do." | Time to first action. What they click first. Who sits still. |
| 2–10 | Nothing. Answer only "what is this?" questions with "find out". | Did anyone find Town Walk or the arcade? What did they say to each other? Any "what am I supposed to do?" |
| 10 | Advance one month from the console. Say nothing. | Did anyone notice? Did anyone open Bills or the pay stub after? |
| 10–18 | Nothing. | Who kept going without being told. Who stopped. What they asked for. |
| 18–20 | Exit slip. | — |

Note names only as counts (did nothing / one action / kept going).

## Exit slip (self-report, no key)
1. What did you do first?
2. What did you want to do that you couldn't?
3. Did anything surprise you?
4. Would you open it tomorrow if nobody told you to? Why or why not?
5. One thing you'd add.

## After: what each result means (plan v2 §4)
- "I didn't know what to do" → wave 1 (town front door, recap) is right as planned.
- "Nothing happened" → pull Crossroads cards (wave 2) forward.
- "It looks boring" → note it; renderer stays last.
- "Fun, what else is there" → School and districts move up.
